Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

1. The Short Version

imgloft processes all images entirely in your browser. We never see your files. We never upload your images to any server. We don't use tracking cookies. We don't collect personal data.

2. How Image Processing Works

All image conversion, resizing, and compression happens client-side using your browser's Canvas API and JavaScript. Your files stay on your device at all times. imgloft has no server-side image processing — there is nothing to upload to.

3. Data We Collect

We don't collect any personal data. Specifically:

  • No images — your files are never uploaded, seen, or stored by us.
  • No accounts — there is no signup or login.
  • No tracking cookies — we don't use advertising trackers, and we run no cookie-based analytics.
  • No personal profile — we can't tie a visit to a person. The only traffic measurement is Cloudflare's cookieless Web Analytics (see section 4), which counts page views without cookies, without a persistent identifier, and without fingerprinting.
  • No logs of your files — we don't maintain server-side logs of visitors or usage, and no image ever reaches a server log because no image is ever sent to one.

4. Third-Party Services

imgloft is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare may collect basic technical data (IP addresses, browser type) for security and CDN delivery purposes, as outlined in Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.

Cloudflare Web Analytics is also active on this site. It is injected by Cloudflare at the edge, not by our page code. It records aggregate page views (page URL, referrer, country, device type) and is cookieless: it sets no cookies, stores no persistent identifier in your browser, and does not build a profile that follows you across sites. We use it only to see which tools people actually use. It never receives your image files — those are processed entirely in your browser and are not uploaded anywhere.

That is the complete list of third parties. There are no ad networks, no social widgets, no external fonts or scripts, and no AI providers receiving your files.

5. Cookies

imgloft does not set any cookies. The Cloudflare CDN may set a __cf_bm cookie for bot protection — this is a technical necessity and does not track you.

6. Contact

For privacy questions, create an issue on our GitHub repository.
